# 24: Session pairing in Day

**What to build:** Day owns the whole session rule. The store reads the log lines in the day's window and hands Day the parsed events (time, unlock or lock). Day picks its unlocks by the day start, pairs each unlock with the lock right after it, and decides open sessions and missed locks. One event type parses and writes a log line, used by `addUnlock`, `addLock`, `day()` and `logEntries()`. Day computes its sessions once (lazy values), since it is immutable.
